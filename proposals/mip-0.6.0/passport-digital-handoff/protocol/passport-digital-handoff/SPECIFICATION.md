# Passport Digital Handoff - Proposed Protocol Amendment

**Target version:** MIP 0.6.0 (draft; beta-only candidate first, public release after acceptance; not part of the published 0.5.0 contract)

Marty creates the ICAO eMRTD digital package through `passport_digital_handoff`. External physical paperwork or later booklet/chip personalization is outside this FlowType. A completed digital handoff MUST NOT assert booklet production, chip personalization, shipment, or credential activation.

## Flow contract

The fixed sequence is `accept_application`, `validate_evidence`, `approval_decision`, `generate_data_groups`, `sign_sod`, `verify_digital_package`, `seal_handoff_package`, `handoff_ready`. The Flow requires active, organization-compatible ICAO eMRTD Credential and Application Templates and a `digital_handoff` Delivery Destination Profile. The Credential Template MUST resolve to the distinct `ICAO_PASSPORT_DIGITAL_HANDOFF` Compliance Profile with `DIGITAL_HANDOFF` issuance protocol; the existing `ICAO_PASSPORT` profile requires physical production and is incompatible. The proposed system profile remains draft and non-discoverable until executable conformance evidence exists. Signing and encrypted artifact capabilities must be available at activation. The implementation MUST use managed document-signer/CSCA issuer profiles for signing; public Flow requests and responses MUST NOT expose issuer-profile IDs, key references, KMS selectors, or secrets.

`verify_digital_package` MUST compare the MRZ source to DG1, validate check digits and ICAO data-group structure, recompute every data-group hash recorded in the exact SOD to be sealed, verify the SOD signature and DSC-to-CSCA chain, and bind the result through protected receipts to the same organization, application, Application Template, Flow execution, and Credential Template. A pass from a simulator, caller-supplied boolean, or unrelated job is insufficient. `seal_handoff_package` MUST encrypt and integrity-protect the exact verified package; any change after verification requires new verification. `handoff_ready` means only that the digital package is ready for an authorized, separately audited physical paperwork process.

The public output is `schemas/passport-digital-handoff-package.json`: a random opaque `handoff_reference`, the digest of an encrypted private manifest, and the digest of its protected signed attestation. It contains no raw or unkeyed digest of MRZ, data groups, SOD, certificates, source job, or artifact. The authorized private view is `schemas/passport-digital-handoff-private-manifest.json`; it is encrypted at rest and never returned by the public endpoint. Schema validation establishes shape only. An authorized independent verifier MUST fetch the protected private manifest and attestation through the opaque reference, authenticate and compare their exact digests with the public response, inspect protected source artifacts and receipts, verify provenance and same-job bindings, recompute MRZ/DG/SOD/chain evidence, and confirm artifact encryption and a passing independent quality check before completion.

Only `approval_decision` is an extensible step for this FlowType. Hooks at `sign_sod`, `verify_digital_package`, `seal_handoff_package`, or `handoff_ready` MUST be rejected at definition activation and execution. An approval hook may receive only the public, redacted Flow context required for that decision; it MUST NOT receive MRZ, biometrics, data groups, SOD bytes, private manifest, issuer-profile or KMS identifiers, custody receipts, or protected artifact references. Hook output MAY veto or inform approval under the configured approval policy but MUST NOT supply, replace, or override the native package verifier verdict, signer result, custody binding, quality-review decision, or handoff state. The native verifier derives its verdict from protected artifacts and trusted receipts and fails closed on any attempted hook-supplied verdict.

## Protected custody, Flow, and quality evidence

The encrypted private manifest MUST record both selected managed issuer profiles as `csca` and `document_signer`, each with an immutable profile ID, positive `registry_revision`, SHA-256 of the RFC 8785 canonical UTF-8 selected profile record at that registry revision, SHA-256 of the exact UTF-8 KMS key reference from that record, SHA-256 of the DER SubjectPublicKeyInfo returned by KMS, SHA-256 of the DER certificate, and SHA-256 of a protected signed custody receipt. `registry_revision` is the organization issuer-profile registry document revision, not a separate per-profile counter; both selected profiles from the same registry snapshot MUST carry that snapshot revision. A later unrelated registry edit does not invalidate the frozen selected profile-record digest. CSCA and document-signer profiles MUST be distinct and organization compatible. The verifier MUST retrieve the selected records from the attested registry revision or an authenticated immutable snapshot, retrieve signed custody receipts from trusted service state, recompute every digest, verify the KMS public key matches the certificate's SPKI, require the private CSCA/DSC certificate hashes to equal their profile-binding certificate hashes, and validate the DSC-to-CSCA chain. The document-signer custody receipt MUST bind its profile record and KMS key to the exact SOD DER digest; the CSCA receipt MUST bind its profile and KMS key to the exact DSC certificate digest. A copied profile ID, metadata-only claim, or signer key selected outside the profile is insufficient.

The private `artifact_seal_flow_attestation_sha256` identifies a protected, signed **artifact-seal Flow attestation**. Step 6, `verify_digital_package`, MUST verify the exact source package and record a trusted native verifier verdict before step 7. Step 7, `seal_handoff_package`, MUST then create the encrypted handoff artifact manifest and its `artifact_sha256` from that verified package. Only after step 7 succeeds, and before step 8, may the Flow authority sign the artifact-seal receipt. It MUST bind the Flow definition ID and immutable version, execution ID, organization, source job, application and Application Template, Credential Template, destination, exact package revision, both issuer-profile bindings, exact SOD DER digest, newly created encrypted artifact digest, native verifier identity and verdict, successful step-7 seal, and timestamp. The private manifest stores the digest of those signed bytes. This receipt proves verification and artifact sealing; it does not prove `handoff_ready` or terminal Flow completion.

After step 8, `handoff_ready`, succeeds, the trusted Flow authority MUST create a separate protected signed **final Flow completion receipt** with the payload in `schemas/passport-digital-handoff-final-flow-receipt.json`. It MUST bind the same handoff reference, private-manifest ID, organization, source job, Flow definition and version, execution, package revision, exact SOD and encrypted-artifact digests, and artifact-seal Flow-attestation digest; it MUST attest all eight steps in exact order and terminal `COMPLETED` status. A receipt from before readiness, a skipped or reordered step, or a merely queued handoff is insufficient. The signed final receipt is stored outside the private manifest and its digest is not included in that manifest.

Only after that final receipt exists may the producer encrypt the private manifest with fresh randomness and create a protected signed **release attestation** with the payload in `schemas/passport-digital-handoff-release-attestation.json`. The release attestation MUST bind the opaque public handoff reference, SHA-256 of the exact encrypted private-manifest bytes, artifact-seal Flow-attestation digest, final Flow-completion receipt digest, trusted signer identity, and signing time. The public `attestation_sha256` hashes the exact signed release-attestation bytes. This order has no circular manifest/attestation hash. An authorized verifier MUST fetch and authenticate all three signed receipts, check signer authority and freshness, compare public and private digests to the retrieved bytes, require their identity/revision/SOD/artifact fields to agree, and reject publication or acceptance unless the final completion receipt proves terminal success.

The independent quality receipt MUST name the exact protected package revision, source job, organization, SOD DER digest, both issuer-profile bindings, decision, reviewer identity, and review time. The reviewer MUST be distinct from the package preparer and CSCA/DSC certificate operators. A self-review, unsigned decision, stale review for an earlier package revision, or caller-supplied quality boolean cannot complete the handoff.

## Evidence encoding and source-job binding

All digest fields are lowercase hex SHA-256. The producer and an independent verifier MUST derive them from the protected source, not from values supplied by a public caller. The source job is a randomly generated UUID retained in private job state. The private `source_job_sha256` is SHA-256 of the ASCII bytes `MIP-PASSPORT-DIGITAL-HANDOFF-SOURCE-JOB-V1`, one zero byte, a four-byte unsigned big-endian length of the UTF-8 `organization_id`, the exact UTF-8 organization ID bytes without case folding or normalization, and the 16 RFC 4122 network-order bytes of the private source job UUID, concatenated in that order. This permits the full nonempty 1..255-character organization ID shape accepted by Flow and FlowExecution; a valid organization reference must still resolve under the deployment's tenant policy. The digest MUST match private `source_job_id`; neither value appears in the public response. The protected, signed issuance and quality receipts MUST bind organization, source job, application, Application Template, Flow execution, Credential Template, destination, exact SOD DER digest, encrypted artifact digest, and handoff reference. A verifier MUST independently recompute the job digest and verify those receipt bindings. The ICAO SOD binds data-group hashes and the document-signer signature; it does not carry Marty organization or job identifiers. The verifier checks SOD-to-data-group integrity separately. A digest alone, or a matching job ID from another organization, is not proof of same-job provenance.

For TD3, `mrz_sha256` hashes exactly the 88 ASCII bytes of the two 44-character source MRZ lines concatenated without a separator, BOM, newline, trailing space, Unicode normalization, or case conversion. `dg1_mrz_sha256` hashes the same 88-byte slice extracted from the decoded EF.DG1 MRZ payload. Both sources MUST already contain only ICAO MRZ characters `[A-Z0-9<]`; the verifier also validates TD3 field layout and check digits. The two digests MUST match. Reconstructed display text or normalized personal-name fields are not the hash input.

`data_group_sha256[DGn]` hashes the exact complete encoded EF.DGn file bytes supplied to SOD generation, including its file tag and length. `sod_data_group_sha256[DGn]` is the lowercase hex encoding of the corresponding 32-byte DataGroupHash value *inside the decoded LDS Security Object*; it is not a second hash of that value. The SOD's declared data-group digest algorithm MUST be SHA-256. The maps MUST have identical keys and values and include DG1 and DG2. A verifier MUST parse the exact DG bytes and SOD, check the embedded hash values and signature, and reject unsupported digest algorithms or any changed data group.

`sod_sha256` hashes the exact DER CMS SignedData bytes emitted by the signer (the bytes represented by the private `sod_der_base64` field), excluding base64 text and any optional EF.SOD file wrapper. The DSC and CSCA certificate digests hash their complete DER certificate bytes. Private `artifact_sha256` hashes the UTF-8 RFC 8785 JSON Canonicalization Scheme encoding of the *complete encrypted artifact manifest*, including its ordered ciphertext `chunks` array, under `marty.passport-artifact-manifest/v1`. It does not hash plaintext, a storage URL, or a serialization chosen by one implementation. The protected encryption, chain, and quality receipts are immutable byte objects identified by their SHA-256 digests; their authenticated content MUST bind the exact package, source job, SOD, artifact, and verifier decision. Retrieval and independent verification of those receipts is mandatory.

The UI issuance service's private package and encrypted artifact manifests may retain `source_job_id`, raw MRZ/data groups, SOD bytes, certificate material, ciphertext chunks, and KMS custody metadata under access control. The shared claims map as follows: private `source_job_id` plus `organization_id` produces private `source_job_sha256`; private source MRZ and decoded DG1 MRZ produce private `mrz_sha256` and `dg1_mrz_sha256`; exact private encoded DG files and SOD values produce private DG hash maps; private signed SOD and certificate DER produce private SOD and chain digests; protected issuer-profile records and custody receipts produce the private `issuer_profile_bindings`; the trusted artifact-seal Flow receipt, signed after step 7 and before step 8, produces private `artifact_seal_flow_attestation_sha256`; and the private encrypted artifact manifest and receipts produce private artifact and receipt digests. The signed final Flow completion receipt remains a separate protected object, created after handoff readiness. The public `protected_manifest_sha256` hashes the *encrypted, authenticated bytes* of the private manifest, not its plaintext or individual fields. The public `attestation_sha256` hashes the exact signed release-attestation bytes. `handoff_reference` is a newly generated, unpredictable UUID used only as an authorization-checked lookup key; it MUST NOT embed tenant, person, document, or storage-path information. The release attestation binds that reference to the protected-manifest, artifact-seal Flow, and final completion receipt digests. The authorized verifier MUST validate all three trusted signatures, signer authority, freshness, digests, and all private relationships. Public digests of ciphertext and signed attestation are not substitutes for this verification.

This FlowType is intentionally TD3 passport-only. The frozen `physical_document_issuance` and physical job contract still support TD1, TD2, and TD3; this amendment neither narrows those routes nor claims digital handoff for TD1 or TD2. Adding other document types requires their own MRZ byte-layout and quality rules before expanding this schema.

## Identifier compatibility

Flow definition and Flow execution IDs, and their `organization_id` projections, retain the nonempty 1..255-character string shape of the existing Flow and FlowExecution schemas. A value must still resolve to the authenticated organization and exact stored Flow definition/execution; syntactic acceptance never bypasses tenant authorization. The protected private manifest and final Flow receipt use the same shape for these references. `credential_template_id` and `application_template_id` must resolve to the UUID IDs of active Credential and Application Templates. The source passport job and its `application_id` use UUIDs as in the existing physical-document job contract. The destination ID retains its existing 1..128-character `[A-Za-z0-9._:-]` shape. `handoff_reference` and the private manifest ID are newly generated random UUIDs; the release attestation carries the same handoff reference.

For the proposed 0.6.0 contract, a Credential Template may reference a system Compliance Profile by its stable `cp-*` or `cpf-*` ID as well as an organization profile UUID. The proposal-local `schemas/credential-template.json` override and `credential-template-passport-digital-handoff.json` fixture demonstrate a valid reference to `cp-icao-passport-digital-handoff`; the matching Application Template, Flow, and destination fixtures share organization and IDs. This resolves a pre-existing schema inconsistency between bundled `cp-*` profiles and Credential Template references without changing active 0.5.0. All referenced objects remain draft in the proposal; beta activation requires their reviewed promotion and compatible active status in the exact beta candidate, with public activation gated separately on the coordinated 0.6.0 release.

## Destination boundary

A `digital_handoff` Delivery Destination Profile is organization scoped and uses `provider: marty_managed`, `delivery_target: secure_artifact`, `credential_format: ICAO_EMRTD`, and `issuance_protocol: DIGITAL_HANDOFF`. It has no external connector. Its destination identifier selects a governed secure artifact location and authorization policy, not a bureau. It MUST NOT be accepted as a `physical_document_issuance` destination.

The existing `physical_document_issuance` FlowType and `physical_production` destination remain unchanged. They make a distinct physical production claim and still require a configured personalization bureau. Implementations MAY add a later physical process, but MUST maintain separate evidence and state transitions.
